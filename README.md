## Profile

Junteng Liu is a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. His research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

### Skills and Research Interests

- Natural language processing
- Machine learning
- LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning
- Hallucination in vision-language models (VLM)
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability

### Education

- Ph.D. in Computer Science, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (2024–Present); HKUST NLP Group; Supervisor: Professor Junxian He
- B.Eng., Shanghai Jiao Tong University (2020–2024); graduated in June 2024; previously advised by Professor Junxian He during undergraduate studies at SJTU

### Honors and Awards

### Research Experience
